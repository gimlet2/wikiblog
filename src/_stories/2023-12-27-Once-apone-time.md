# Once upon a time

## Chapter 1
That was one of this lazy days when Christmas is over already 
but still there is a plenty of time till the New Year.
That period of year when every your colleague is on vacations and nobody but your family needs your attention.

As you probably know a typical day might have a what we call morning(unless you've slept right till the lunch). And that day has morning too. And that morning I've spontaneously decided to spend with a cup of nice coffee in one of my favorite coffee places in Berlin. Public transport is one of the things that works well in Berlin. Most of the time - if there is no storm or strike. And that is default way for me to commute. But not today. If you've decided to be spontaneously then be spontaneously. And I've drove instead.
