# Projects

A collection of small web projects by RestMonkeys.

## [Still, Somewhere — Film Photographs](https://photo.restmonkeys.com)

A film photography journal featuring quiet moments and travel photographs made on 35mm and medium-format film.

## [Reading](https://abc.restmonkeys.com)

A browser-based reading tool with several modes, including letters, words, and stories.

## [Blood Pressure Monitor](https://bp.restmonkeys.com)

Record blood pressure and pulse measurements, review their history, and set reminders.

## [Expense Tracker](https://expenses.restmonkeys.com)

Track expenses by category, review spending analytics, and import or export your records.

## [HAM Deutschland Exam Prep](https://ham.restmonkeys.com)

Practice exam questions, with controls to browse all questions or filter by section.

## [Minesweeper](https://minesweeper.restmonkeys.com)

Play Minesweeper in your browser.
